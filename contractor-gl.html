Before you continue

This quote is for a claims-made General Liability policy, not occurrence. Coverage applies only if a claim is first made against the insured and reported while the policy is in force, for injury or damage that occurs after the retroactive date shown on the quote.

Coverage continues only while the policy stays in force. Renewing with the same carrier keeps the original retroactive date. If the policy is cancelled, non-renewed, or lapses, or the insured moves to another carrier, later claims are not covered unless an Extended Reporting Period is purchased or the new carrier honors the retro date.
